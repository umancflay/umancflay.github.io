Delete your data — Toy-Nado: Triple Match 3D

Developer: Voo Game Studio

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to voogamestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Toy-Nado data deletion".
  2. We reply within 30 days and confirm what was deleted.

You can also delete all game data yourself at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Toy-Nado → Storage → Clear data.

What is deleted

  • Game progress, coins, boosters, collection and local scores (stored only on your device).

What may be kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law; we do not store them ourselves.
  • Advertising data is held by Google AdMob. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or use Google's own privacy tools.
